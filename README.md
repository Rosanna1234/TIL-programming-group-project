Project Group 32

## Research question
Which sections of national highway in South Holland are unsafe given how busy they are, and which characteristics are associated with this?

We combine accident data (BRON) with traffic volumes (INWEVA) from Rijkswaterstaat to calculate the accident risk per carriageway section: the number of accidents per million vehicle-kilometres. This lets us find road sections that are dangerous, not just busy.

## Subquestions
1. How does the ranking of carriageway sections by number of accidents differ from the ranking by accident risk?
2. To what extent does the number of accidents increase with vehicle-kilometres, and which characteristics are associated with more accidents than expected?
3. Are the sections with the highest risk of injury and fatal accidents the same as those with the highest risk for all accidents?
